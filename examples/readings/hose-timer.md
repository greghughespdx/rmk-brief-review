# The back-tap hose timer

## What happened

The timer on the back tap failed open twice in December. Each time it ran the
drip line for about nine hours instead of twenty minutes, and the raised bed
flooded.

| Date | Should have run | Ran for |
|---|---|---|
| December 4 | 20 minutes | about 9 hours |
| December 19 | 20 minutes | about 9 hours |

## The replacement

The same maker sells a two-outlet model for about 40 dollars. It fits the same
tap thread and keeps the same schedule buttons.

## Why now

The town's summer watering ban starts June 1. A timer that fails open during
the ban is a fine, and the next failure is likely before then.
