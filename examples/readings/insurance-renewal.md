# House insurance renewal

## The renewal

The policy renews on March 1. The insurer's letter says the premium will be
"reviewed", and the new figure arrives in mid February.

## Last year

The paper reminder arrived three days after the due date. The policy lapsed
for two days before the payment cleared.

## The two payment options

1. **Automatic payment** from the joint account on the renewal date. It can be
   cancelled online at any time, and a failed payment sends an email.
2. **Manual payment** after the paper reminder, as today.

The paper reminder can stay on with either option.
