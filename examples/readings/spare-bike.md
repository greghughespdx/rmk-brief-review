# A winter home for the spare bicycle

The spare bicycle stands against the garage door track, so the door stops a
foot short of fully open.

## The three places with room

| Option | Cost | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| A. Wall hook in the garage | about 15 dollars | Two screws into the stud beside the shelves. |
| B. The shed | nothing | The lawn mower has to move to the back wall. |
| C. Sell it | brings in about 80 dollars | A listing and one evening for the pickup. |

Nobody has ridden it since last spring.
